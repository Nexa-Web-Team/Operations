02Account Requirement
Hard Requirement · IT/Operations
All NEXA work on Claude — regardless of seat type — must be performed on an @digitalnexa.com account. No exceptions.
Authorised
@digitalnexa.com account
The only authorised environment for any Claude activity connected to NEXA clients, projects, or internal operations. Access is revoked automatically when an employee leaves.
✓ Eligible for Team plan invitation
Not Permitted

Any non-@digitalnexa.com account may not be used for NEXA work. Company data on personal accounts remains accessible after an employee leaves — this is the core data risk this policy addresses.

03Migration to the Team Plan
How the invite works: IT/Operations sends a Team plan invitation to your @digitalnexa.com address. On accepting, you are prompted to bring your existing data into the Team workspace — chats, projects, files, memory, connectors, and skills all move across. Any active Pro or Max subscription cancels automatically with a prorated refund. The move is one-way. You have seven days from the invite, or can act later via Settings > Account.
Path A
Already using a @digitalnexa.com individual account
Straightforward environment change. Migrate at the point of acceptance.
Step 1
Receive Team invite
IT/Operations sends an invitation to your @digitalnexa.com address. A reminder banner appears in Claude for seven days.
Step 2
Accept and choose migration
On accepting, select the option to bring your data into the Team workspace. Chats, projects, files, memory, connectors, and skills move across automatically.
Step 3
Subscription cancels automatically
Any active Pro or Max subscription cancels with a prorated refund at migration. No manual action needed.
Done
Fully onboarded
You are on the NEXA Team plan with existing work intact. The individual account is retired for all NEXA activity.
Path B
Currently using a personal email account for NEXA work
Requires a @digitalnexa.com account first. Invite issued once IT/Operations confirms readiness.
Step 1
Create @digitalnexa.com account
Register a new Claude account at claude.ai using your @digitalnexa.com email if one does not already exist.
Step 2
Transfer NEXA work manually
Recreate NEXA-related prompts, projects, and custom instructions in the new company account. Chat history cannot transfer — logs remain on the personal account.
Step 3
Notify IT/Operations
Confirm to IT/Operations that the @digitalnexa.com account is ready. The Team plan invitation is then issued to the company email.
Step 4
Accept invite, cease personal account use for NEXA
Accept the Team invite. From this point, no NEXA Claude activity may take place on the personal account. Continued use is a breach.
Done
Fully onboarded
You are on the NEXA Team plan. The personal account may continue for personal use only — no NEXA activity permitted.
IT/Operations Checkpoint
For Path B, the Team plan invitation is not issued until IT/Operations confirms the @digitalnexa.com account is active and NEXA work has been transferred.
No exceptions
04Breach & Consequences
Consequence Ladder
Enforced from first instance
Using any personal account for NEXA Claude activity after the migration deadline is a serious policy breach. All breaches are recorded by IT/Operations and escalated to the relevant line manager and HR.
First breach
Formal written warning. Employee must confirm in writing that all NEXA work has ceased on the personal account. Warning retained on record.
Repeated breach
Escalated to formal HR disciplinary process. May result in termination of employment depending on severity and circumstances.
Aggravating factors
Breaches involving client data, proprietary methodology, or deliberate circumvention are treated as immediately serious regardless of prior warning history.
